Profile

I am a musicologist with a PhD from Stellenbosch University (2025) and a former IBM senior consultant and enterprise architect. I coach people to write and communicate clearly in English: IT professionals who work in English as an additional language, and postgraduate students, especially ESL students, who are writing a thesis. I have written examined research in both Afrikaans and English, and I have worked on IT projects in South Africa, Singapore and Nigeria.

“… meets and in certain respects exceeds the scholarly standards required for the conferral of the PhD degree.”  PhD examiner’s report, Dr P.A. Schonken, Stellenbosch University

Coaching services

1. ESL coaching for IT professionals. Practical professional English for people who work in IT: writing architecture and design documents, specifications, reports and emails, and explaining technical ideas clearly in meetings and presentations. It draws on a decade as a senior consultant and enterprise architect (2007–2018) and on co-authoring an IBM technical book.

2. One-on-one thesis writing coaching. For honours, master’s and doctoral students, especially those writing in English as an additional language. I coach how the argument is built: the research question, the structure and logic of the thesis, academic English style, and referencing conventions (Stellenbosch-Harvard, APA, MLA and Chicago). The work stays the student’s own. I do not edit or write for students, and I do not replace the supervisor.

3. Group thesis writing coaching: The Thesis Hourglass. A four-week online writing studio for music postgraduates, in groups of four. Each week a student writes one sentence of a three-sentence thesis argument (the wide opening, the narrow middle and the wide base). I mark each sentence and we rewrite it together in a live session. Free pilot places open from 19 January 2027.
