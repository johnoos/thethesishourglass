It does not say Mini-MBA. Check that the CV wording matches the certificate.

- **"Three major peer-reviewed theses".** The theses were examined, not peer-reviewed in the journal sense. Consider the wording.
- **Not on the CV but evidenced in the PDFs:** Open Group Master Certified IT Architect (2008) and the UNCG exchange semester (2019-20).
- **Article date.** The CV says June 2026. Confirm the current publication status.
